05 / User permissions

Access follows responsibility.

Subscription features answer “what the workspace owns.” Roles answer “who inside it may do what.” Platform staff remain in a completely separate access realm.

Access-model note: these are the approved role boundaries. Permissions attached to later-phase features activate only with those features; current security and tenant-isolation rules remain authoritative.
OW

Workspace Owner

Final commercial and ownership authority.

Full workspace, brand, content, publishing, analytics, team, billing and credit access.
Only role that transfers ownership, deletes the workspace or changes plan/payment.
AD

Workspace Admin

Runs the workspace day to day.

Manages settings, team, brands, content, publishing, social connections and analytics.
Cannot transfer/delete ownership or change plan/payment; billing is view-only.
MM

Marketing Manager

Owns strategy and execution.

Brand Brain, AI strategy, campaigns, content, approvals, publishing, analytics and automations.
Team administration is limited to non-admin roles.
CC

Content Creator

Builds and schedules content.

Creates drafts and AI content, manages assets, comments, submits and schedules own approved work.
Cannot publish externally, approve work, manage team or billing.
CW

Copywriter

Owns written content.

Text drafts, AI writing, own assets, comments, approval submission and own-content analytics.
No publishing, visual generation, approvals or administration.
DS

Designer

Owns visual expression.

Visual Brand Brain suggestions, creative drafts, AI creative, assets and submission.
No publishing, approval, strategy or administration.
AP

Controls quality before publish.

Reviews, approves or rejects, comments, views analytics and uses Copilot to explain.
Cannot create, schedule, publish or manage the workspace.
AN

Turns results into decisions.

Views and exports analytics, comments, reads aggregate credits and brand-scoped activity.
No content mutation, publishing, approval or billing changes.

Reads assigned brand information, comments and limited analytics where enabled.
No Copilot or mutations. Existing RBAC role—not sold as an agency feature.

Highest platform authority.

All platform operations, financial activation, users, roles, configuration and security.
High-impact actions require audit and step-up controls.

Runs platform operations.

Customers, workspaces, plans, features, integrations, AI routing, support and configuration drafts.
Cannot manage platform users; financial configuration activation remains owner-controlled.
SA

Helps customers safely.

No secrets, configuration, plan changes or unrestricted customer writes.
BM

Handles financial operations.

Assigns plans, manages trials and credits, billing events, refunds and financial exports.
No AI routing, general configuration, platform users or customer content.

08 / Top-ups & usage policy

No overage.
No surprise invoice.

When the included balance reaches zero, AI actions stop clearly. The rest of the workspace continues to function while the customer upgrades or buys a prepaid pack.

0

Hard stop

No postpaid overage on any MVP plan. An AI action cannot create a charge the customer did not authorize.

+

Prepaid packs

Purchased before use and valid for 12 months. Pack sizes and USD/SAR prices remain pending Phase 4 economics.

↻

Controlled rollover

Monthly plan credits roll over only up to one monthly allowance. Nearest-expiry credits are consumed first.

Top-up price status

Not approved yet—and deliberately not invented.

Pack sizes and prices must be derived from the real blended provider cost, payment fees, target margin and expected model mix measured by Phase 4.

Input 01
Provider cost per taskMeasured across the chosen text, image and strategy models.
Input 02
Required gross marginOwner-approved margin floor for plans and packs.
Input 03
Customer simplicityThree clear packs at most; no provider or token vocabulary.
09 / Commercial guardrails

Pricing can change.
Trust cannot.

The commercial model is configuration, not hard-coded product behavior. Existing customers retain the terms and resources they were promised.

01

Prices are versioned

Changing a catalogue price does not silently reprice an existing subscription.

02

Downgrades do not delete

Resources above a new limit become read-only and return after re-upgrade.

03

Human approval

Financial configuration is drafted and activated through separated duties.

04

Every movement is auditable

Grants, reservations, charges, releases, expiry and adjustments remain traceable.
