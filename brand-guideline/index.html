03 · Logo system

One mark. Strong recognition.

The BrandSpace mark is intentionally compact, digital-first and product-friendly. Protect its proportions and give it enough space to remain confident.

BrandSpace
BrandSpace
1×1×1×1×
Clear space & size

Give the mark room to breathe.

Use at least one quarter of the symbol width as clear space on every side. Recommended minimum digital size: 24px for the symbol and 104px for the full lockup. Use the symbol alone for favicons, avatars and compact UI.

Do not stretch or compress the logo.
Do not rotate or tilt the mark.
Do not add decorative shadows or outlines.
Do not recolor the approved master mark.

06 · Visual language

Soft surfaces. Strong ideas.

The BrandSpace visual system is built around generous whitespace, soft depth, rounded geometry, abstract light and a small number of deliberate accent moments.

Surfaces

Rounded, layered, calm.

Use 18–32px radii for major containers. Prefer soft fill and spacing hierarchy over heavy borders.

Depth

Subtle, never glossy.

Use broad, low-opacity shadows. Shadows should separate surfaces, not decorate them.

Photography

Real, editorial, useful.

Avoid generic corporate stock imagery. Prefer product context, real teams, clean environments and intentional crops.

Illustration

Abstract intelligence.

Use simple geometric forms, soft gradients and data-inspired structures. Never use cliché robot or glowing-brain imagery.

Icons

Simple line geometry.

Use one consistent icon family. Icons support meaning; they should not become decorative noise.

09 · Voice & messaging

Smart without sounding robotic.

BrandSpace speaks like an experienced operator: clear, calm, useful and direct. It should sound confident enough to guide, but never overclaim.

Do

  • Use plain language and short, purposeful sentences.
  • Explain what happens next.
  • Make AI behavior transparent and reviewable.
  • Use specific product language: brand, strategy, content, publish, learn.
  • Sound intelligent without using jargon for status.

Don't

  • Do not say “revolutionary”, “game-changing” or “magic”.
  • Do not personify AI as all-knowing.
  • Do not use hype to replace evidence.
  • Do not sound like a generic social media agency.
  • Do not promise actions that are not actually available.

11 · Motion

Quiet confidence in motion.

Motion exists to clarify relationships and signal intelligence. It should never compete with the task.

Reveal

Use 180–320ms ease-out for cards and page content. Small vertical movement only.

Hover

Use 1–4px lift or subtle shadow changes. Avoid exaggerated scaling.

AI processing

Use soft pulse, sequential dots or connected-node activity. Avoid neon “energy” effects.

Reduced motion

Respect system preferences and preserve all information without animation.

12 · Accessibility

Brand expression never outranks usability.

Brand Purple and Brand Yellow are identity colors, not default text colors on white. Use accessible text tokens and clear interactive states.

✓
ContrastUse Ink or approved dark tokens for body text. Do not use yellow as body text on white.
✓
FocusEvery interactive control must have a visible keyboard focus state.
✓
MeaningNever communicate status using color alone; include text, shape or iconography.
✓

✓
TypographyMaintain readable line lengths and comfortable body line-height on every breakpoint.
✓
