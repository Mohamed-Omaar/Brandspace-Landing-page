# BrandSpace customer platform prototype

This route is an interactive product-spec prototype for the **final customer-facing BrandSpace dashboard**. It is intentionally not a visual-design sign-off and does not call a backend.

## Purpose

Use `/prototype/` to review the complete customer journey, module boundaries, buttons, expected actions and hand-offs before Phase 8 / launch work is finalized.

The prototype follows the platform product definition and keeps the original customer modules in scope, including **AI Content Studio, AI Creative Studio, Asset Library, Social Calendar, Social Publishing, Analytics, AI Copilot and Automations**.

## Core cycle represented

Brand Brain → AI Strategy → Campaign → AI Content Studio → AI Creative Studio / Assets → Approval → Social Calendar → Social Publishing → Smart Analytics → Marketing Intelligence / learning → improved Brand Brain.

## Important scope notes

- AI Creative Studio is represented as an original product module: AI image generation + on-brand adaptation / templated resizing.
- Media can be uploaded, selected from Asset Library, or generated in AI Creative Studio, then attached to a content variant and carried through preview, approval, calendar and publishing.
- Video may exist as a customer-uploaded Asset; AI video / voice generation is not represented as an MVP capability.
- Copilot is permission-aware and shows confirmation before mutating/external actions.
- AI actions show the intended credit-governance model (estimate → reserve/execute/settle conceptually); this prototype only simulates it.
- This is the **customer dashboard** prototype only. Public marketing site and Platform Admin are intentionally not mixed into this route.
