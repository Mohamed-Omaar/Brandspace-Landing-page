10 · Do / Don't

Keep the feed recognizable.

The goal is a disciplined system that can scale. Avoid creating a new visual language for every post.

DO
  • Lead with one idea per post.
  • Use white or soft surfaces most of the time.
  • Keep purple as the primary accent.
  • Use yellow selectively for attention.
  • Maintain strong margins and short copy.
  • Reuse the same 4:3 template families.
DON'T
  • Fill every post with gradients or effects.
  • Use stock photography just to fill space.
  • Turn each feature into a different campaign style.
  • Overuse bold type or all caps.
  • Use yellow for long text.
  • Add unnecessary icons, borders or decorative noise.
