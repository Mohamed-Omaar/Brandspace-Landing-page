17 / Help & troubleshooting

Know what to check first.

Most blocked actions are explained by one of four things: role, brand scope, plan entitlement or connection health.

I cannot see a brand or post.

Confirm the workspace and brand switchers, then ask an Owner or Admin to check your membership’s brand scope. BrandSpace returns a not-found view for data outside your authorized scope.

A button or action is missing.

Check your assigned role and the workspace plan. A role cannot bypass a plan entitlement, and a plan cannot bypass a role restriction.

AI generation will not start.

Check the credit balance, feature access, Brand Brain requirements and any moderation message. The expected credit cost must be accepted before a chargeable task runs.

A scheduled post did not publish.

Open the post details and inspect approval status, account connection health, platform validation and publish status. Follow the specific reconnect, edit or retry instruction shown.

I received an invitation error.

Invitation links are single-use and time-limited. Ask an authorized workspace member to cancel the old invitation and send a new one.

What happens if we downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Resources above the new limit become read-only until the workspace upgrades again or moves within the lower limit.

When contacting support: share the workspace name, screen, approximate time and the request reference shown in the interface. Never send your password, social login or full secret value.
